## What the proposed conditional diagnostic establishes

For this diagonal squashed Gaussian, the joint log density is the sum of eight coordinate log densities. Let `ell_i(s)=E[log pi_i(a_i|s)]`. Under the existing target entropy−8, define `p_global=sum_i ell_i−8`; under hypothetical coordinate targets−1, define `p_i=ell_i−1`. Then **`p_global=sum_i p_i`**. In the production `−alpha*p` temperature loss, positive pressure would increase log-alpha under gradient descent; negative pressure would decrease it. The alpha factor changes magnitude, not this sign.

Opposite signs between one coordinate and the sum are mathematically possible. A scalar constraint controls total expected entropy; it does not impose eight entropy floors. That is a property of the objective, not automatically an implementation bug or impaired behavior. Here these pressures are conditional on selected recent grasp states, whereas the production temperature uses replay minibatches. The frozen actor is also not the historical behavior actor.

The planned fixed 16-noise estimate can report the predeclared ≥75% descriptive fraction, the identities above and per-coordinate margins. Compute **squashed-action** log densities with the exact tanh Jacobian, not latent Gaussian entropy or post-clipped densities. Average samples within each state before classifying its pressure. Report pooled pressures too: their signs can differ from the majority of statewise signs. Sixteen samples can make near-zero signs uncertain; report margins/Monte Carlo uncertainty rather than treating every sign as exact. The 1,904 states come from 64 correlated training episodes; they are not independent population trials. A passed snapshot gate admits no temperature allocation algorithm or robot experiment.

## Primary literature

[Haarnoja et al., *Soft Actor-Critic Algorithms and Applications*](https://arxiv.org/html/1812.05905), Section 5, Appendix C and hyperparameter table: automatic temperature is derived from a constraint on expected **joint** entropy; the practical heuristic is `−dim(A)`. Appendix C gives the elementwise tanh density correction. This directly supports the scalar-versus-coordinate distinction, but does not prescribe a per-coordinate remedy.

[Kim and Sung, *An Adaptive Entropy-Regularization Framework for Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning*, ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/kim23v/kim23v.pdf), methods, experiments and discussion: ADER learns differing exploration targets across agents using disentangled pure-return/entropy values. The paper explicitly suggests treating action dimensions as agents. Its experiments include a staged two-action toy, multi-agent HalfCheetah and cooperative multi-agent tasks. The toy requires one coordinate to explore while the other exploits, then reverses their roles; fixed unequal targets do not solve it. This is relevant prior art for allocation, not validated single-arm squashed-entropy-collapse repair or tenfold manipulation evidence.

[Xue et al., *FastDSAC*, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.12612), Section 3.1, ablations 4.3 and appendices L/M/O: Dimension-wise Entropy Modulation scales each base standard deviation by `w_i=N*softmax(logit/tau)_i`. It deliberately suppresses variance in precision-critical joints and shifts exploration elsewhere. Results span humanoid/manipulation benchmarks; most use three seeds, with five on two hard tasks. Its target-entropy and temperature ablations are task dependent, and distributional critics/other configuration changes complicate direct transfer. It is neither eight learned temperature duals nor a coordinate-floor guarantee. As a separate algebraic caveat, `sum w_i=N` fixes weight sum, **not total variance or squashed entropy**, since `sum w_i²*base_sigma_i²` varies. Standard SAC already has independently predicted coordinate standard deviations.

The clean conclusion is that entropy allocation is established prior work and can matter. The evidence also cautions against forcing noise into a gripper or precision-critical coordinate. The snapshot should expose the current global/coordinate tradeoff, including potentially beneficial low entropy; it cannot determine which entropy targets would improve lifting, identify critic accuracy, or authorize another learned method.
