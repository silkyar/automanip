## What the broader reading changes

**PPO tuning is too narrow a research program.** Strong from-scratch alternatives improve the information obtained from each transition through replay, different targets, imagination, representations or exploration. But algorithm names hide major differences in learner work and deployment. The next decision should follow an observed bottleneck, not familiarity with PPO. [REDQ](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.05982), [TD-MPC2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.16828v2), [PPG](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/cobbe21a/cobbe21a.pdf).

**World models have credible manipulation evidence, but not under our exact contract.** TD-MPC2 includes ManiSkill2 Stack Cube, with a different controller/action interface and longer horizon, and plans at every action. Training-only short model branches, as in MBPO, could preserve a feed-forward deployment actor; they still require a competent replay comparison and contact-model validation. [TD-MPC2](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.16828v2), [MBPO](https://arxiv.org/html/1906.08253).

**More updates and more useful updates are different claims.** REDQ is an important no-model comparison to MBPO. RUDDER changes reward attribution; Retrace changes off-policy targets; PPG changes feature transfer and policy preservation. Our extra-critic negative result does not test these mechanisms. It only rejects the tested recipe. [REDQ](https://arxiv.org/html/2101.05982), [RUDDER](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2019/file/16105fb9cc614fc29e1bda00dab60d41-Paper.pdf), [Retrace](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2016/file/c3992e9a68c5ae12bd18488bc579b30d-Paper.pdf).

**Retention and adaptation compete.** Protecting an actor can stop collapse while freezing learning. Resetting a learner can restore fitting capacity while destroying useful behavior. BC-to-online literature supplies several explanations—action support, value calibration, coverage—not a diagnosis of our own collapse. [AWAC](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.09359v6), [Cal-QL](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.05479), [primacy bias](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/nikishin22a/nikishin22a.pdf), [EWC](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.00796).

**The exploration question is about complete useful sequences.** Independent action noise, coherent noise, episodically committed policies and returning to a frontier are distinct. Go-Explore's state restoration is an extra privilege; a learned return policy must earn its route through counted interactions. Smoothness alone is not proof of more efficient task acquisition. [Bootstrapped DQN](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.04621), [gSDE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2005.05719), [Go-Explore](https://adrien.ecoffet.com/files/go-explore-nature.pdf).

## Mechanism map for our observations

These are hypotheses and proposed discriminating observations, not an experiment queue.

| Candidate explanation | What would distinguish it | Relevant literature and caution |
|---|---|---|
| Useful trajectories are too rare | Before policy updates, compare phase progression and recovery under equally scaled exploration; establish an actual coverage deficit | Coherent exploration / goal-directed discovery. Does not follow from a flat success curve alone. |
| Useful experience is discarded or its credit remains poor | Measure target quality and temporal credit on retained training sequences; compare corrected targets with equally expensive ordinary reuse | Retrace, RUDDER, EBU, HER. Our short episodes already have GAE and dense shaping. |
| Accepted updates destroy useful behavior | Hold collected data and update direction fixed; vary displacement, including a small reverse-direction control | TRPO, PPO, Adam, PPG. Small KL or preserved success alone can mean no learning. |
| Early fitting makes later learning harder | Compare matched-target fitting ability and Q/target scale at early versus late checkpoints before resets | Primacy, ReDo, plasticity and value-divergence counterevidence. Dormancy or a plateau is insufficient diagnosis. |
| Poor predictive features limit generalization | Test whether action-conditioned predictive features improve downstream decisions over equal-cost generic auxiliary fitting | SPR, successor representations, world models. Prediction loss is not task competence. |
| A learned dynamics model could replace real experience | Establish useful short-horizon contact predictions and policy improvement beyond strong equal-compute replay | MBPO, TD-MPC2, Dreamer. Count model learning, generated rows and planning. |

The strongest broad opportunity is **obtaining multiple valid improvements from scarce useful experience**. That could ultimately require replacing PPO. The highest-value immediate diagnostic is narrower: separate a bad policy-improvement direction from a damaging update size, using our observed BC-to-PPO collapse. Choosing that diagnostic does not claim it will yield 10× efficiency, or that assisted retention explains delayed from-scratch learning.

The [scientific diagnosis and one experiment proposal](../research-strategy-diagnosis-20261028.md) connects this map to the existing ledger and gives falsification criteria. All prepared training work remains disabled pending review.
