# Temporal abstraction after the release diagnostics

This focused follow-up supplements the earlier survey. It does not count abstracts as full-paper reviews or authorize implementing every method. Our own randomized data distinguish reaching a stack briefly from maintaining it; the current causal test isolates renewed closing and arm motion after release.

**Option-Critic, Bacon, Harb and Precup (2017).** Read the option definition, call-and-return execution and intra-option/termination gradient derivations. It jointly learns option policies and termination from task return rather than supplied subgoals or demonstrations. This is directly relevant to a learned corrective behavior that must persist after the original policy briefly succeeds. It does not establish that hand-designed maintenance is a learned option, or that adding an option hierarchy automatically helps our task. [Primary paper](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/10916/10775).

**Q-chunking, Li, Zhou and Levine (2025; v4 May2026).** Read the chunk-space TD argument, offline-prior exploration construction and compute appendix. Conditioning a critic on the executed action sequence avoids a particular multi-step off-policy mismatch; prior behavior supplies coherent sequences. Its main setting is offline-to-online, with substantial offline training and data. Reported runs take4–7hours on RTX-A5000, and Robomimic runs8–12hours. Those online curves cannot be treated as from-scratch or compute-matched evidence for our ten-minute setting. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.07969v4).

**SEAR, Nagy et al. (March2026 preprint).** Read method, principal ablations and hyperparameter/evaluation appendices. This is more relevant to from-scratch learning: multi-horizon causal critics and randomized replanning make chunked online learning work without prior data in their experiments. Naively chunking a standard learner performed poorly. Their final-step success criterion also distinguishes stable completion from a fleeting success. Experiments use1Minteractions, UTD1, batch256 and substantial512-wide actor/transformer-critic networks. The paper does not establish equal wall time or compute to our PPO. Replanning every single step is not their best execution setting, which matters because our frozen actor interface currently executes one action per observation. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01891v1).

Our inference: persistence may be a useful action-level hypothesis, but correlated noise, a learned chunk, and a corrective option are different mechanisms. The earlier AR-noise result was negative; it did not test learned termination or post-success maintenance. A successful manual maintenance probe would justify investigating how to learn that behavior, with the source-policy prefix and any supplied action prior fully counted. It would not justify a new from-scratch efficiency claim.

Before a chunk-based implementation, test its essential issue in a small controlled problem: whether longer decisions improve discovery and credit while still permitting contact-time corrections. Measure primitive interactions and learner work, including failed exploration and prefixes. Preserve a single-step comparator and distinguish any assisted learning. Before deploying a new policy representation, it must fit the restricted safe artifact/evaluation contract; this reading is not permission to modify frozen evaluation code.
